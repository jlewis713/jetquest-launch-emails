Let's talk about money, because most people never realize how much of this trip they do not have to pay for.

Two things you get just for being here, then three ways to earn on top.

What you start with
Your $200
Finish your Boarding Pass, get approved, and $200 in trip credit is yours. Any trip. Never expires. That happens before you refer anybody.
Your friends get the same $200
Anyone who joins through your link gets that same $200 when they are approved. You are not asking a favor. You are handing them the head start you got.
What you earn
$100 when your 4th friend is approved
One time. The trigger is your friends getting approved, not booking. Once four of them are in, $100 in credit lands in your account.
10% of the trips you are not on
When someone you referred books a trip you are sitting out and pays in full, 10% of what they paid comes back to you as credit. Every time it happens.
4 friends on your own trip and it is free
Get four referred friends fully paid on a trip you are going on, and your own seat cost comes back to you in credit. The whole seat.
On any single trip you earn either the 10% or the free seat, not both.

So picture four friends joining through your link, and all four coming to Rio with you. Your $200 is already sitting there. The fourth approval adds $100. Then your own seat cost comes back. You spend New Year in Brazil with four people you actually chose, and the trip has paid for itself.

None of it expires. It just waits for you to pick a destination.
